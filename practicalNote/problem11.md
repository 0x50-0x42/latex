# Adding transparent PNG files

* Got a lot of fake (see [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/LaTeX/comments/sp2jrz/images_with_a_transparent_background/)) transparent PNGs.
* To convert them to transparent PNG, use [https://picsart.com/background-remover/png-maker/](https://picsart.com/background-remover/png-maker/).
